# Errata: claims in the issues that are withdrawn, corrected, unconfirmed or superseded

The issues and `docs/archive/` are the history of this research and are not rewritten. Where a claim there was later withdrawn,
corrected, never confirmed, or replaced by a decision, it is listed here with the current reading and where that comes from. **Before
quoting any number or decision from an issue, check this page and the [catalog](INDEX.md)**; the catalog is the current state.

Every issue listed here carries a banner at the top of its body that points to its rows, and the label `has-errata`.

Status words:
- **withdrawn**: shown wrong or retracted; do not use.
- **corrected**: replaced by another number; use the current value.
- **unconfirmed**: a measured number that does not carry the claim made of it (one unpaired run, different exes, a development set,
  a pre-fix build); quote it only with its caveat.
- **superseded**: a plan, rule or decision replaced by a later one.

A new contradiction found goes here at once, with a banner on its issue, in the same turn as the fix to the docs (`AGENTS.md`, "The
docs are the source of truth").

**The ones agents quote most**
- inlineclose 41/48, 43/48, "0 regressions on 96 traces", "0 destructive false alarms": withdrawn (XS-28 to XS-36, MR-7, MR-8).
- Sidecar results and the latent controller-bundle results, the one-token-window claim: withdrawn (X276 rows; `learned.md`, `detect.md`).
- "0 wrong of 51,015": corrected to 0 of 39,046 for the best-config arms (`compute.md`).
- Third-repeat close (+43.7 % tokens without it) and the Gyro-M `(exact)` note (+26.6 %): unconfirmed, different exes / one unpaired run;
  the note is replaced by the plain value (plan D3).
- The engine's 49/49 repeat: measured greedy only (XS-42, MR-32).
- Qwen3.6-first and profile-first plans: superseded by "Qwen3.5-4B first" and "General Style first" (plan D4, `AGENTS.md`).
- GPQA greedy gains and other greedy wrong-to-right claims: superseded by the sampled pairs (MS rows).
- `--hint-calc` 5 vs 3: withdrawn, the programs never ran (MS rows).

160 rows in all.
